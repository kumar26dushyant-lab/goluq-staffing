Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 August 2026

GoLuQ builds software and sets up business communication services — WhatsApp Business API, toll-free numbers, voice and SMS. This page explains, in plain language, what we collect when you use goluq.com or message our WhatsApp number, why we collect it, who else can see it, and how to have it deleted.

The short version. We do not use advertising cookies, we do not store your IP address, and we do not track you across other websites. We keep what you deliberately give us — your name, your number, and what you asked for — so a real person can answer you.

1. Who we are

GoLuQ (goluq.com) is a software and business communication practice based in India, operated by Dushyant Sharma. For anything on this page, contact dushyant@goluq.com or WhatsApp +91 83495 04400.

What we deliberately do not collect

  • No advertising or tracking cookies, and no third-party advertising pixels.
  • No IP addresses stored with page views.
  • No full referring URLs — we store only the referring site's domain, because a full URL can carry personal information in its query string.
  • No cross-site tracking, and no selling of anyone's data to anyone, ever.

3. If you message us on WhatsApp

Our business number runs on the official WhatsApp Business Platform, provided by Meta. When you message it:

  • Your message, your number and your WhatsApp profile name reach us and are stored so we can reply and keep the thread together.
  • You may first be answered by our automated guide. A person can take over the conversation at any time, and does so when you ask for one.
  • Meta processes the message in transit under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • If you ask us to stop — "stop", "not interested", or similar — we mark the conversation closed and do not message you again.

5. Where it is stored, and for how long

Data is stored in a database on our own server in Europe, and in the services listed above. We keep enquiries and conversations for as long as we may reasonably need them to serve you and to keep our own business records — and we delete them on request, as below. Customer and partner accounts are kept for as long as the relationship lasts.

6. Your choices, and how to have your data deleted

You can ask us at any time to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. There is no form and no charge.

  • Email dushyant@goluq.com with the subject Delete my data, or
  • WhatsApp +91 83495 04400 and say so.

Tell us the phone number or email address you used, so we can find the right records. We will action it within 30 days and confirm when it is done. Some records may be retained where the law requires it — for example invoices — and we will say so if that applies.

To stop WhatsApp messages immediately, reply STOP to any message from us.

7. Cookies and browser storage

We set no advertising cookies. The site keeps a few small values in your own browser's storage so it behaves sensibly: your chosen language, whether the guide's voice is muted, a random session identifier used only to group page views, and — if you have a customer login — your sign-in token. These stay on your device and are not shared with anyone. Clearing your browser storage removes them.

8. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Passwords, where accounts exist, are stored only as salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes and never in a readable form. Access to the administration area is restricted to the owner.

9. Children

GoLuQ sells to businesses. The site is not intended for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.
