# Getting real clients: the targeted, free-first roadmap (2026-09-20)

Budget now: ₹0 for four weeks of work, ₹500 for one measured test. Paid
only if the free machine, run properly, brings nothing measurable. Every
door already exists (WhatsApp, Telegram, /start, the calendar, partners);
this roadmap is about pointing real people at them, one targeted list at a
time, and measuring what comes back in the cockpit.

## The three targets, in order of how fast they pay

| Target | Who, exactly | Why they buy | Door |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Partners (India)** | CAs, insurance and finance advisors, distributors' salesmen, market-association secretaries, WhatsApp-group admins in Indore, Bhopal, Ujjain, Dewas | A second income with zero investment; they already get asked "who builds this?" | /partner, the five-step short, the fifteen cards, the kit |
| **Owners (India)** | 10–100 staff: coaching institutes, distributors, clinics, CA firms, garment wholesalers, real estate, schools, salons, logistics | The 9 pm call, dues collected, six rents, the number that walks out with a salesman | /start (written plan), WhatsApp, the films |
| **Owners abroad (Gulf, AU/NZ, UK)** | Indian-origin traders and professionals, plus locals needing a system built to their operation or a WhatsApp Store for export buyers | Fixed price in their currency, own the code, built on their own cloud account | /start with `?c=XX`, Telegram door, LinkedIn |

Partners come first because one partner is a warm channel to fifty owners,
and the programme costs nothing until an order is paid.

## Week 2 · Partner drive and communities

6. **Twenty partners in Indore.** The list: ICAI Indore members you know,
   LIC and private insurance advisors, the secretaries of the cloth,
   electronics and grain market associations, admins of trader WhatsApp
   groups, college placement cells (final-year commerce students). Message:
   "Open your GoLuQ partner office, free; here is the five-step film." Each
   sign-up gets the kit automatically; the Monday brief shows sign-ups and
   ref-coded leads.
7. **Facebook and WhatsApp groups, value first**: Indore business, MP
   traders, CA and coaching-institute groups. One film a week per group,
   posted as a story ("this is what a nine-pm call costs"), never as an
   advert; reply to comments with the plan link.
8. **Quora and Reddit answers** (English, international): five answers a
   week to "how do I get a WhatsApp catalogue for my business", "custom
   software vs SaaS for a small distributor", with the relevant film and the
   `?c=` link for the asker's country.
9. **YouTube done right**: titles that match searches ("WhatsApp Store for
   garment wholesalers", "Software for coaching institutes in Hindi"),
   playlists per industry, every description ending in the plan link. The
   library is already uploading daily.

## Week 3 · Abroad, and the ₹500 test

10. **Gulf and AU/NZ through people, not ads**: Indian trade associations in
    Dubai (IBPC), Sharjah, Sydney, Auckland; Indian-origin CAs and
    accountants there; LinkedIn groups for Indian entrepreneurs abroad. The
    partner deal travels: a Dubai CA can be a partner exactly like an Indore
    one. Telegram door on every creative for the Gulf and CIS.
11. **The ₹500 test**: boost the best-performing organic post (by
    cockpit clicks) on Facebook and Instagram for five days, ₹100 a day,
    audience: Indore + Bhopal, 25–55, interests small business / business
    owner, placements Reels and feed. Success line: at least 3 plan requests
    or 2 partner sign-ups with the campaign's UTM in the cockpit. Below
    that, paid stays off and the free loop continues.

## Week 4 · Measure and decide

12. Read the cockpit: Visitors by source and country, Leads by door and
    ref code, Briefs requested, partner sign-ups. Keep what brought a lead,
    double it; stop what brought none. Then decide on a paid step, in this
    order if ever: Google Search ads on "WhatsApp business software" +
    city (intent, not reach), then Meta lead ads to the partner short.

## What we say, everywhere

- One promise, in the visitor's language: "Tell us what slows you down;
  you get a written plan today and a fixed price on a 30-minute call."
- Worked examples, never claims. No client names until a client agrees.
- Partners: "a share of every order, monthly on managed customers; exact
  terms on the call." Never a percentage in public.

## Measured in the cockpit

Every link carries `utm_source` and a door tag; Leads show the ref code;
Visitors show country and source; the Monday brief reports leads by door.
The first real client is expected from the partner drive or the warm
WhatsApp loop, not from search; search pays from month two.
