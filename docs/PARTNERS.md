# Partner programme — mechanics and outreach (2026-09-15)

The pitch (owner's words): **open your GoLuQ.com partner office**. No stock,
no staff, no tech. You know business owners; we build what they need and you
earn on every order you introduce and on every enhancement they add for two
years. Never a rate in public creatives; the live terms are on goluq.com/partner.

## What a partner is paid (the model that exists in code)
- A share of GoLuQ's **profit** on each project they introduced (price minus
  delivery cost), credited as the customer pays, paid by UPI once approved
  balance ≥ ₹500. Rate, enhancement window, typical margin, minimum payout
  and attribution window are cockpit settings (`aff_*`), currently 20 %,
  24 months, 40 %, ₹500, 90 days.
- Indicative figures used on cards, derived from those settings: a ₹50,000
  order at a 40 % margin → ₹4,000; a ₹1,50,000 order → ₹12,000. Cards say
  "indicative" and point to the live terms.
- Not in the model: a share of managed-plan (monthly) fees. The owner's
  "recurring revenue" line is honoured through enhancements for two years.
  If monthly plans should also earn, that is a settings + accrual change,
  not a copy change — decide before promising it.

## How attribution works end to end
1. Partner link `goluq.com/?ref=CODE` → stored 90 days in the browser
   (last click wins) → any lead form or /start brief carries `ref_code`.
2. WhatsApp click-to-chat `wa.me/918349504400?text=Hi GoLuQ, ref CODE` →
   the first message stamps the thread with the code.
3. Telegram `t.me/GoLuQ_client_bot?start=CODE` → thread stamped.
4. When the owner creates a project for that customer, `partnerForCustomer`
   finds the code on the lead; commission accrues only on `recordPayment`.

## The kit (dashboard → "Your partner kit")
- Code, share link, WhatsApp deep link with the code, downloadable QR.
- Ten cards to forward (EN or HI by the partner's language).
- Three ready messages: WhatsApp status/groups, Facebook/Instagram caption,
  direct message to a shop or clinic owner — each with the partner's link.
- Four steps, no jargon.

## Creatives
- Six partner cards EN + HI at goluq.com/catalog/aff_*.jpg (office, network,
  share, earn, steps, kit); in the Publish picker. Reel (15 s, EN/HI) still
  to make: "Open your GoLuQ partner office" — same before/after engine.

## Outreach plan for partners
- Who: shop owners, CAs and advisors, insurance/real-estate agents, students,
  ex-colleagues, anyone with a business network in tier-2/3 India and the
  Gulf diaspora.
- Where: the 30-day social queue gets one partner card a week; LinkedIn post
  in week 3; WhatsApp broadcast to existing contacts who opted in; the
  founder's own network first (the first ten partners are always people you
  know).
- Message: "Open your GoLuQ partner office" + one card + the /partner link.
- Onboarding: register (2 minutes) → dashboard with the kit → first share the
  same day → owner's Telegram alert on every partner registration and every
  lead with a ref code.

## How prospects reach us fastest (distribution of the doors)
- Every card, reel end-frame and post carries the WhatsApp number and, for
  Telegram-first markets, the QR to @GoLuQ_client_bot.
- Click-to-chat links name their door in the prefilled text ("from Facebook",
  "from QR", "ref CODE"), so the cockpit and the Monday brief can say which
  door produced enquiries.
- The guide answers on WhatsApp, Telegram and the site within seconds, 24×7,
  in the customer's language; the owner is alerted on Telegram on every new
  conversation and can take over from the phone. That is the "quick
  response": no form, no wait, one tap into a live thread.
