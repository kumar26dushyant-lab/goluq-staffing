# Video testimonial — Ashwin Kaushal, MD, Nidaan Legal India LLP

Two versions of the same 120-second piece: English, and Hinglish (the one most
Indian business owners will actually watch to the end).

**Rules built into both scripts**
- The words "AI", "bot", "chatbot" and "artificial intelligence" appear nowhere.
  He talks about automation, digital systems, and processes — which is what he
  actually experiences.
- Every claim is one Mr Kaushal can make from his own experience. Nothing about
  our internals, nothing he would have to take on trust from us.
- Specifics beat adjectives. "Four offices on one system" is worth more than
  "excellent service", and it is the line a viewer remembers.

**Filming notes** — send these with the script:
- Phone camera, landscape, at eye level. Not held in the hand — prop it up.
- Sit facing a window; never with a window behind him.
- Office background, not a blank wall. His own desk is ideal.
- Record in **three separate takes** (the ✂ marks). Short takes are easier to
  deliver and far easier to edit than one long one.
- Ask him to say the sentence again if he stumbles — do not stop the recording.
- 120 seconds is roughly 300 words spoken naturally. Both scripts are close to
  that; tell him to speak in his own words rather than reading. A script read
  word-for-word looks read.

## Version 1 — English

**✂ Take 1 — who he is, and the problem (about 35 seconds)**

> I am Ashwin Kaushal, Managing Director of Nidaan Legal India LLP.
>
> We handle insurance claims across India — four offices, and a network of
> doctors, advocates, surveyors and claim experts working with us. For a long
> time all of that ran on phone calls, spreadsheets and WhatsApp messages. If I
> wanted to know where a claim had reached, I had to ring someone and ask. Every
> new office made that harder, not easier.

**✂ Take 2 — what changed (about 45 seconds)**

> Dushyant Sharma and GoLuQ built us one system that runs the whole operation.
> Every office, every role, every claim, in one place — and it works on the
> phone, not only on a computer.
>
> That is the part I did not expect. My entire office is on my mobile now. I can
> open it anywhere and see exactly where each claim stands, who is working on it,
> and what is pending. My team enters what they do as they do it, so the picture
> is always current. Nobody has to prepare a report for me.
>
> Customer communication moved to WhatsApp and it simply works — updates go out
> on their own, and people get answers without waiting for someone to be free.

**✂ Take 3 — the result (about 40 seconds)**

> The difference is in the numbers. We have settled more than two thousand
> claims, with a success rate above ninety-five percent, and we did it without
> adding the staff we thought we would need. The work that used to take a full
> day of follow-up now happens by itself.
>
> What I value most is that Dushyant builds it himself. He understood how our
> business actually works before he wrote anything, and when we need a change, it
> is done — not put in a queue.
>
> If you are running an operation on spreadsheets and phone calls, talk to
> GoLuQ. It changed how we work.

## Version 2 — Hinglish

**✂ Take 1 (about 35 seconds)**

> Main Ashwin Kaushal, Managing Director, Nidaan Legal India LLP.
>
> Hum poore India mein insurance claims handle karte hain — chaar offices hain,
> aur doctors, advocates, surveyors, claim experts ka pura network hai. Pehle yeh
> sab phone calls, Excel sheets aur WhatsApp messages par chalta tha. Kisi claim
> ka status jaanna ho to kisi ko phone karke poochna padta tha. Har naya office
> khulne se kaam aasan nahi, aur mushkil ho jaata tha.

**✂ Take 2 (about 45 seconds)**

> Dushyant Sharma aur GoLuQ ne humein ek hi system bana kar diya, jismein poora
> operation chalta hai. Har office, har role, har claim — sab ek jagah. Aur sabse
> badi baat, yeh mobile par chalta hai, sirf computer par nahi.
>
> Aaj mera pura office mere mobile mein hai. Kahin se bhi khol kar dekh sakta
> hoon ki kaun sa claim kahan pahuncha hai, kaun kaam kar raha hai, kya pending
> hai. Team apna kaam wahin update karti hai, to picture hamesha latest rehti
> hai. Mujhe report mangwani hi nahi padti.
>
> Customer communication WhatsApp par shift ho gaya hai, aur bilkul smooth chalta
> hai — updates apne aap chale jaate hain, aur logon ko jawab ke liye intezaar
> nahi karna padta.

**✂ Take 3 (about 40 seconds)**

> Farak numbers mein dikhta hai. Do hazaar se zyada claims settle ho chuke hain,
> success rate pandrah… sorry, pichyanave percent se upar hai — aur yeh sab bina
> utna staff badhaye jitna humein lagta tha ki lagega. Jo follow-up pehle poora
> din leta tha, ab apne aap ho jaata hai.
>
> Sabse achhi baat yeh hai ki Dushyant khud banate hain. Unhone pehle samjha ki
> humara kaam actually chalta kaise hai, phir kuch banaya. Aur koi change chahiye
> ho to ho jaata hai — line mein nahi lagta.
>
> Agar aapka business abhi bhi Excel sheets aur phone calls par chal raha hai, to
> ek baar GoLuQ se baat kariye. Humare kaam karne ka tareeka hi badal gaya.

> **Note for the Hinglish take 3:** the "pandrah… sorry, pichyanave" is a
> deliberate prompt to say *ninety-five* (पिच्यानवे), not *fifteen*. Remove it
> before sending — it is here so whoever briefs him gets the number right.

## What to ask for alongside the video

- **One still photo** of him at his desk with the system open on his phone. That
  single image will do more work across the site and social posts than the video,
  because most people scroll without sound.
- **Written permission** to use his name, designation, firm and likeness on
  goluq.com and on social media. One WhatsApp message saying so is enough, and it
  matters — a named testimonial you cannot prove consent for is worse than none.

## Where it will be used

- `/` homepage, below the proof strip
- `/services`, against the WhatsApp and toll-free cards
- LinkedIn, as the anchor post — this is the audience that buys multi-branch
  platforms
- Cut into 3 vertical clips of ~30s for WhatsApp Status, Instagram and Facebook
