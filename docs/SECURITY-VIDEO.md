# "How we secure your data" — the two-minute video, per region (2026-09-17)

Purpose: trust, in the owner's own words. One owner, one customer, one
salesman. No jargon. The same three promises as goluq.com/security. Made
with the reel pipeline (scenes from the image model, voice-over, captions),
one cut per region: India (Hindi + English), Gulf (English, Arabic captions),
Australia/New Zealand (English). Only the people, shop and money change.

## Script (India, English — Hindi is a direct translation)

**Scene 1 — the fear (0:00–0:20).** Evening, a garment wholesale shop.
Owner Rakesh watches his best salesman, Amit, scroll through customer chats
on Amit's own phone. Voice: "Forty buyers. Their prices, their habits, their
trust. All inside a phone that walks out of the shop every evening."

**Scene 2 — promise one (0:20–0:45).** Next morning. Same customers, now
messaging the shop's own WhatsApp number. Amit answers from the shop's
system; the owner sees every chat on one screen. Voice: "With GoLuQ the
customers talk to the business, not to a phone. Amit still replies. But if
Amit leaves, the buyers stay." Caption: *Your number. Your customers.*

**Scene 3 — promise two (0:45–1:10).** The owner's screen: three logins.
Owner: everything. Amit: his buyers. Accountant: invoices only. Voice:
"You choose who sees what. Nobody else gets a login. Every change says who
made it and when." Caption: *Only the people you name.*

**Scene 4 — the plumbing, briefly (1:10–1:35).** A padlock over a server
icon; a shield with the WhatsApp mark. Voice: "Everything is stored
encrypted and backed up every night. WhatsApp runs on Meta's official
business platform — the same one banks use — with every message signed. No
key or password ever sits in the software itself." Caption: *Encrypted.
Official. Locked.*

**Scene 5 — promise three (1:35–1:55).** Owner and Dushyant on a call. Owner:
"And if I ever leave?" Dushyant: "You take everything — your data, your
code. We delete our copy." Caption: *You can always leave. That is why you
can trust staying.*

**Scene 6 — close (1:55–2:05).** The shop, calm, the owner leaving at seven.
Voice: "GoLuQ.com. Built for you to own." End-frame: WhatsApp number, QR to
the Telegram bot, goluq.com/security.
