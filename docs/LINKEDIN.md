## 12. Outreach playbook and the honest maths

Target buyer: owner, MD or operations head of a 10–100-person business in
India, UAE, UK, Australia, US: distributors, clinics/hospital groups,
coaching chains, CA/law firms, real estate, logistics, schools, small
manufacturers. Search LinkedIn by title + industry + city; Sales Navigator
(₹8–9k/month) makes this 5× faster and is worth it from month one.

Daily routine (45 minutes):
- 20 connection requests with a one-line note that names their business,
  not ours: "Saw you run a distribution business in Pune — we build the
  reminder and ordering systems distributors run on. Happy to connect."
- 5 follow-ups to accepted connections, 2–3 days later, one question:
  "Quick one — who sends your payment reminders today, a person or a
  system?" Then the relevant reel. Then the offer of a 30-minute scope call.
- 2 thoughtful comments on posts by target buyers (visibility without ads).

The maths at a USD 3–5k ticket, from typical B2B outreach rates:
- 400 requests/month → ~120 accept → ~30 reply → ~8 scope calls → 2–3 wins.
That is the target; it needs the routine every working day, not bursts.
First month is usually 0–1 wins because the pipeline is empty; months two
and three reach the target if the daily numbers hold.

What makes the ticket USD 3–5k: sell the complete system (CRM + billing +
dashboard, or WhatsApp Office + reception + reminders), never a single
feature. The scope call ends with a written plan (goluq.com/start makes it
before the call) and a fixed quote in the buyer's currency.

Paid: once 10 organic posts are up, boost the two best-performing to the
same audience (title + industry + country) at USD 5–10/day for two weeks;
judge by scope calls booked, not likes.
